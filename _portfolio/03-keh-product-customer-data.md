## The work

At KEH, I worked on both sides of a retail data problem: making product information usable across systems and turning customer activity into better decisions.

I led an overhaul of the product information model for 100,000 SKUs, using data pipelines and integrations to bring information from multiple sources together. I also helped implement RudderStack data collection for more than 20 million monthly website events in BigQuery, then used segmentation to support marketing decisions.

In parallel, I built Python and Google Cloud models for price and inventory optimization using demand, seasonality, and price elasticity. I led the move from Universal Analytics to GA4 and developed dashboards and pipelines for marketing analytics.

## The result

The product data became more useful for downstream systems, and the customer analysis gave marketing a clearer view of its audiences. I presented the findings to KEH's CEO and marketing leadership.

**Tools:** Python, SQL, BigQuery, RudderStack, GA4, Qlik Sense, Google Cloud, GraphQL
